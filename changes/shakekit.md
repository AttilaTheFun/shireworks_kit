- ShakeKit: `.shakeToReport(repository:)`, `ShakeKitCommands`, `ShakeReport`. A shake (or the menu command) opens the composer with a screenshot of the moment, and files the issue with the app's and device's details and its redacted, capped logs. It files with the host's shared sign-in when it reaches far enough, else ShakeKit's own. IssueComposer: an empty draft clears the store instead of being saved.
