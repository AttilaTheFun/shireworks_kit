- Updates on a Mac: an app with no handler of offers answers with the kit's update sheet ("<App> <version> is ready", Update Now or Later), in place of quitting at once; Later asks again an hour on. Offers are looked at every five minutes and at each activation too, besides the signal. An offer can be a person's (`makeOffer(…, byPerson: true)`, `UpdateOffer.byPerson`, migration 5), which replaces a waiting, deferred, declined or failed offer of the same build. `UpdateSystem` gains `showUpdateSheet(_:)` and `activations()`, with defaults (Update Now; none). README's "Adopting" says so (docs/updates/Updates.md, "Updating automatically").
