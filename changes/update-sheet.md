- `.shireworks()`: the one modifier an app adopts the kit by, on its root view (ShireworksKit). It starts crashes and updates once a process (`ShireworksKit.start()`, now idempotent, still there for processes without SwiftUI views), puts shake to report on the view, filed in the Info.plist's `ShireworksRepository` (or `.shireworks(repository:)`'s), and, on a Mac, answers Shireworks' offers. README's "Adopting" says so.
- Updates on a Mac: an app with no handler of offers answers every offer, automatic or a person's, with the kit's update sheet ("<App> <version> is ready", Update Now or Later), in place of quitting at once; Later asks again an hour on. Offers are looked at every five minutes and at each activation too, besides the signal. An offer can be a person's (`makeOffer(…, byPerson: true)`, `UpdateOffer.byPerson`, migration 5), which replaces a waiting, deferred, declined or failed offer of the same build. `UpdateSystem` gains `showUpdateSheet(_:)` and `activations()`, with defaults (Update Now; none). A registered `AppUpdates.register(autoUpdate:)` still answers in place of the sheet (docs/updates/Updates.md, "Updating automatically").
