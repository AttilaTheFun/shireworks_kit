- Report a Bug opens at once: a shake (or Help › Report a Bug…, `ShakeKitReportButton`) showed the sheet only after the app's logs were read and the screenshot made a PNG, on the main actor, two to three seconds with an app's usual logs. Now only the screenshot's image is taken first; its PNG is made, and the logs read, after the sheet opens and off the main actor, the logs matched by `OSLogStore` itself (a predicate: 0.8 s in place of 2 to 3). The sheet says "Reading the app's logs…" until they're in (`ShakeReport.isReadingLogs`), and Create waits for them and the screenshot, 15 seconds at most, then files without the logs, saying so. `IssueComposerModel.preparing` is what submit awaits before uploading.
